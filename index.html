A few good things to know.

Where exactly is Sea Rocket?

We're on the hill behind Whitby station in a quiet residential area. The town and harbour are about 5–10 minutes away on foot, and the beach roughly 10 minutes. The walk back involves a hill.

Can we bring a dog?

Yes, one small-to-medium dog is welcome. We can provide a dog bed and bowls on request.

Is Sea Rocket good for families?

Absolutely. We can provide a travel cot, high chair, baby bath, children's cutlery, toys and books on request. The property has stairs across multiple floors.

What about parking and arrival?

Free on-street parking is available nearby but spaces aren't guaranteed immediately outside the house. Self check-in is available with a lockbox. Check-in is after 4pm and checkout is before 10am.

Can I book directly online?

For now, send us an enquiry and we'll confirm availability and a quote personally. An integrated availability calendar and secure online checkout are planned.
