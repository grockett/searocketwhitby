01 / A coastal day out

A little further.
A little slower.

Some days are best left open: a walk to look forward to, something good in the picnic bag, and no hurry to be anywhere else.

A day on the coast is as much about the pauses as the miles: a picnic, a view, a moment to watch the sea. From Whitby to Robin Hood’s Bay, make room for the journey.

03 / One stage at a time

Make a day of it.

Tap a stage to open its walking notes. You can keep more than one open. Estimates allow 3–4 km/h and exclude stops, café detours and village exploration.

Walking stages

Along the way: the icons highlight food & drink and photo opportunities.

  1. 01Steps, church & headlandWhitby to Saltwick BayDistance 1.8 kmEst. time 30–40 minFood & drinkPhotos
    1. Start at the 199 Steps and climb towards St. Mary’s Church. The church and Whitby Brewery Tap Room are the first two places marked on the map.
    2. Pick up the Cleveland Way signs on the headland and follow the coast towards Saltwick Bay, keeping to the signed path and any marked diversions.
    3. The Saltwick Bay waypoint marks the end of this stage on the coast path. Pause for the view; exploring the beach is a separate outing.
    Link to stage 1 ↗
  2. 02A landmark on the coastSaltwick Bay to Whitby LighthouseDistance 1.8 kmEst. time 30–40 minPhotos
    1. Leave the Saltwick Bay waypoint on the signed Cleveland Way towards Whitby Lighthouse. Follow the coast path rather than a route down onto the shore.
    2. Keep the lighthouse as your next landmark. This stage ends on the recorded path beside it, approximately 3.6 km into the walk.
    3. Take a moment here before the longer stretch towards Robin Hood’s Bay. Continue on the signed coast path for the next stage.
    Link to stage 2 ↗
  3. 03Northcliffe & the village approachOn to Robin Hood’s BayDistance 7.2 kmEst. time 110–145 minFood & drinkPhotos
    1. From Whitby Lighthouse, continue along the signed Cleveland Way towards Robin Hood’s Bay. The coastal section beside Northcliffe Holiday Park is a useful landmark further along.
    2. For an optional break, The Coast Cafe Bar is inland at Northcliffe Holiday Park on Bottoms Lane, beside the Cinder Track. Use the café’s access information and check opening hours; this detour is not part of the measured walk.
    3. Continue on the signed Cleveland Way to the final recorded point at the approach to Robin Hood’s Bay. Follow local signs for any diversion rather than leaving the path to match the track.
    4. The village waypoint lies beyond the track finish. If you head down into the old village for food or a look around, allow time for the climb back to the bus stops at the top.

04 / Before you set off

Good to know.

A few practical details to make planning easier.

Underfoot

A coastal walk with steps and climbs. Keep to signed paths, follow local diversions and stay back from cliff edges.

What to bring

Wear walking boots or shoes with good grip. Bring drinking water, waterproofs and an extra layer.

Weather & the coast

Check the forecast before setting off: coastal fog and strong winds can change the feel of the day. Read the National Park’s walking advice ↗

Tides & access

The guide follows the supplied coastal track. For any separate beach visit, check tide times and heed local signs; do not treat the shore as a shortcut. Coastal safety advice ↗

05 / The way home

And back to Whitby.

Leave a little room in the day for the journey back. A final pause, something to eat, and time to make your way home.

Plan the return

Arriva’s X93/X94 corridor connects Robin Hood’s Bay and Whitby. Check the operator’s live timetable for the service running on your day.

For the return to Whitby, look up the Thorpe Lane stop in upper Robin Hood’s Bay and select the Whitby-bound service. If you explore the lower village, allow time to walk back uphill.
