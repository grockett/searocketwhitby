01 / ARRIVING

First things first.

Welcome to Sea Rocket. Here are the two things to know when you arrive and when it's time to head home.

Check-inFrom 4pm

Come in from 4pm. Your key is in the lock box beside the front door. The code is included in your automated check-in email.

Check-outBy 10am

When you leave, return the key to the lock box and scramble the numbers. There's a short check-out list below.

02 / SETTLE IN

Get comfortable.

Everything you need to make the house feel like home.

Wi-Fi

The Wi-Fi is fast and available throughout the house. Scan the QR code in the printed house folder to connect. If that doesn't work, the network name and password are printed there too.

Heating

The central-heating thermostat is on the shelf in the living room. It's a learning thermostat and should keep you comfortable. To change the temperature, simply rotate the dial. Please leave it plugged in.

Music

You'll find a Bluetooth speaker on the living-room shelf. Press the large round control to switch it on and pair with Ruark Mrx. If you're on the Wi-Fi, you can also use Spotify Connect, Tidal, Deezer or Amazon Music and connect to Grove Groove. Please be considerate of the neighbours when choosing the volume.

Television

The living-room TV pulls out for a better viewing angle. It has the usual apps and live broadcast channels, and you can also cast from your own device.

03 / IN THE KITCHEN

Cook, eat, enjoy.

A few notes on the appliances, plus the kit for taking lunch out into the fresh air.

Oven & induction hob

The oven is electric and fan-assisted. Baking sheets and roasting trays are in the corner cupboard; you'll find a selection of pans there too.

On the oven, the first dial from the left controls the grill. The second selects the oven mode — for the usual fan setting, turn it two places anticlockwise. The third dial sets the temperature. The four hob rings are operated by the four dials on the right-hand side.

Microwave

Use the centre button in the row of three buttons above the control knob:

  1. Press the centre button once.
  2. Turn the knob to set the power.
  3. Press the centre button again to set the time.
  4. Press the button with the play icon.
Dishwasher

The dishwasher is in the cupboard to the right of the sink. Tablets are under the sink.

  1. Load the dishwasher.
  2. Hold On/Off for three seconds.
  3. Select a programme.
  4. Hold Start for three seconds.
  5. Close the door.
Picnic kit

There's plenty to explore nearby, so we've put together a few things for picnics. You'll find them at the bottom of the tall cupboard:

  • Picnic blanket
  • Plates, bowls and cups
  • Picnic cutlery
  • A flask
  • A cool bag

Please clean everything and return it to the cupboard after use.

04 / THE PRACTICAL THINGS

Laundry & everyday essentials.

For longer stays, muddy walks and the occasional bit of housekeeping.

Washing machine & drying

The washing machine is in the utility, next to the fridge-freezer. It has the usual range of programmes. There's a washing line with pegs on the shelf above, and a clothes airer in the wardrobe of the first-floor front bedroom.

Vacuum cleaner

The handheld Dyson hangs on its charger above the shelf over the washing machine. Pull the trigger to use it; there's also a turbo mode for heavier dirt. The brush attachment is on the charger. The long tube and floor head are next to the dishwasher.

Iron & hairdryer

You'll find an iron, ironing board and hairdryer in the wardrobe of the first-floor front bedroom.

05 / OUTSIDE & PARKING

A little fresh air.

Make use of the courtyard, and a few pointers for bins and parking.

The courtyard

There's a table and chairs in the courtyard for a morning coffee or an evening chat. The outside light is operated by the switch next to the door. Please be considerate of the neighbours if you're out there late at night.

Bins & recycling

The indoor bins are in the cupboard to the right of the dishwasher. The rear bin is for recycling; the front bin is for general waste.

The larger bins are in the alley behind the property, through the blue gate in the courtyard. The green bin is for general waste; the blue bin is for mixed recycling — paper, cardboard, glass, plastic containers, cans and cartons. Please don't put plastic bags in the recycling.

Parking

There is free on-street parking on Grove Street and the streets immediately around Sea Rocket. At busy times, finding a space anywhere in Whitby can be difficult. Two long-stay car parks — Marina and Endeavour Wharf — are a short walk down the hill.

06 / BEFORE YOU GO

Checking out.

We hope you've had a lovely and memorable stay. Please check out by 10am and run through this short list before you leave.

By 10am, please…
  • Close all windows.
  • Empty the bins.
  • Turn off the music and television.
  • If you've loaded the dishwasher, put it on using one of the tablets provided.
  • Lock the doors, return the key to the lock box and scramble the code.

We'd love to hear how your stay went. Your feedback helps us make Sea Rocket better. If anything comes up during your stay, please message us through your booking platform or email searocketwhitby@gmail.com.

In an emergency

Make every effort to leave the property safely and quickly. Only once you're safely outside should you call the relevant emergency services. Please don't try to fight a fire yourself. Once everyone is safe and the emergency services have been alerted, let us know through your booking platform or by email.

Fire safety
  • The fireplaces have been blocked. Please don't light any fires.
  • No smoking or vaping anywhere on the property.
  • Please close the kitchen and utility doors at night to help contain the spread of fire and allow more time to evacuate.
  • Heat and smoke alarms are fitted on each floor and in each bedroom. If an alarm sounds, evacuate everyone unless it is clearly for a known reason, such as burnt toast.
  • There is a fire blanket next to the cooker for cooking fires that can be tackled easily. An extinguisher is in the tall cupboard; only use it to help you escape from the building.
  • Emergency lights are plugged in on the ground floor and first-floor landing. They can be unplugged and used as torches if needed.
First aid & emergency numbers

A basic first-aid kit is on the top shelf of the tall kitchen cabinet.

  • Emergency services: 999
  • Non-emergency police: 101
  • Non-emergency medical advice: 111
